Capoeira Angola

An introduction to Capoeira Angola through its history, music, and movement — a living tradition carried for generations.

What other art form or practice do you know of that combines martial art, dance, music, poetry, healing, ritual, folklore, acrobatics, improvisation, strategy, bio-mimicry, intercultural connection, and spirituality; and is even inclusive to anyone regardless of age, ethnicity, religion, or identity. This is the beauty of Capoeira Angola, the Afro-Brazilian freedom fighting art form that was and is still used to this day to liberate and empower the bodies, minds, spirits, and communities of its practitioners from oppression and struggle. You will often see it's practitioners full of joy, smiles, and laughter, and among them, the art is called a "jogo" or game, where Capoeira is played.
Lev has been a devoted practitioner of the art for over a decade primarily as a student of Mestre Cobra Mansa, as well as other teachers along the way most notably Ras Tre Subira, Mastre Skher Brown, and ContraMestre Stefan Bonitz. During that time, Lev has taught and worked with Baltimore City Youth, performed with the Dancing Warriors, a Capoeira Edu-tainment Group at Baltimore City Schools, and is raising his own children with Capoeira Angola as a way of life. Workshops are customized to the group, taking age and experience level into account and invite the participants in to a new world in which they can explore a new way of moving their bodies, connecting with friends, and a cultural richness that transcends language barriers and body types.
Book a Capoeira Angola Experience for a memorable one of a kind taste of this rich cultural art that will leave you feeling connected, empowered, and enlivened to yourselves, to each other, and to nature itself.

Earth Skills

Natural incense harvesting and bundling: to walk around the forest and not only see what can be used for food or tools, but to see the potential of something to be burned — and to find plants you can turn into cordage to bundle your incense. From the moments where there's a smell we don't like, to the moments we want to bring in a smell we do — the smoke is a powerful tool, and knowing how to make fragrant wreaths from the Earth around you is a really powerful skill to have.

Palmetto weaving: a skill you can find in many situations, not just with palmetto fronds but with any plant that grows a certain way — weaving baskets, hats, and mats.

Wild herb foraging walks: we love to learn the land, the green allies that nourish us and give us what we need throughout the day — an experiential walk discovering what's in the environment we're sharing, even using some of these herbs in teas and meals in the moment.

Teeth Wellness

Through many hard lessons and some deeply celebrated successes, we have realized how to begin transitioning from toxic-laden fluoride and misunderstanding around nutrition to re-mineralizing our teeth in simple ways with tools we can have on hand. We're not acting like we know everything — but we have gone through the trenches and come out having re-mineralized, paused decay, and witnessed it not returning. We continue to study with the dentists and wise people carrying forward the work of Weston Price, and we can teach you what we have learned.

Solutions, offered

Tools

Every tool that we have here, we either have access to or have experienced — and can tell that it could be significantly beneficial for an individual or a community effort toward the w.i.s.e values we intend to support in the world.

Wellspring Infrastructure

An infrastructure we utilize to support the wellness of our body, both internally and externally, and also to clean our spaces.

We believe it is the evolution of a relationship with water. Just as we have gathered around water since the beginning of our journey on this planet, we believe this tool could be pivotal in reclaiming those gathering spaces — and supporting each other to recognize the ways we can impact each other's lives to save money, become more resourceful, and ultimately heal this planet.

Freeze Dryer

A piece of infrastructure that has been really helpful for feeling a deep sense of security in times when we really don't know what could happen in the future in relationship to the food we have. Whenever we experience abundance, we do everything we can to make that abundance last — and this is a tool that can make it last the longest and maintain the most nutritional value. We recommend it, have utilized it immensely, and intend to continue to do so.

Analema Water Wand

A tool at the leading edge of the evolving science of studying water. Very simple, and much more accessible than our wellspring infrastructure — and at the same time a very profound technology that can help us reclaim some of the deepest qualities of water in our life, by something so simple as a wand placed in the water.

When we began to understand the ways that water is impacted by our thoughts and actions — and by the chemicals and aggression toward this planet — we realized how important it is to reclaim the wisdom and intelligence that is always accessible in water. This wand brings the strongest energy possible into the water through their wonderful year-long technique of seeding the water with the beneficial energies of the Earth. In this way we can reclaim our waters not only structurally, but energetically as well.

Every tool we offer ultimately supports the larger work: gathering your community around you, and supporting yourselves to feel home on this planet — in a way that ultimately impacts and is impacted by all.

Rates & Exchange

GATHER works on the same principle as everything we do: reciprocity, spoken out loud. We can talk about cost directly and early.

The shape of the work

This work is project-based. Each gathering is priced as itself — the workshop, the performance, the full arc of what we’re making together — not by the hour. Time is a tool here, not the measure.

Flexible forms of exchange

We're open to a range of ways to be paid:

  • Bartering
  • Fiat
  • Crypto
  • Gold & silver

Sometimes entrance into the event is payment enough. A full room of the right people is its own kind of wealth — we can talk about what you bring versus what you pay.

Ways we can gather

Workshops

Priced per gathering and group size, scaled to who’s in the room and what we’re building.

Performances

A single rate for the piece itself, agreed before the doors open, so the art is never haggled over.

Co-created events

Longer collaborations priced as a whole arc — workshop series, residencies, gatherings with a life of their own.

Rates vary by workshop, and that’s on purpose — each gathering asks something different. Bring what you have; let’s find the exchange that lets everyone arrive feeling well.
