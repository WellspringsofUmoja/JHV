B3 W.i.s.e

B3 W.i.s.e is my (Ife ShepsuMa'at's) main solo offering in Journey Home Vitals. It organizes my studies and passions over the last 20 years into something that I can offer that feels whole in it's entirety, sequential in its progression, and complete in its individual offerings.

There are three ways to engage this work — and they can build on each other:

The First B

Base Clarity

Orientation, breath, mind — discovering and nurturing your felt sense of wellness from within.

 The Second B

Bridge Competence

Boundaries, branding, & broadcast — carrying your clarity into the public world, on your terms.

 The Third B

Bloom Courage

Status, equity, stewardship — standing fully in public spaces with your self-referencing wellness honored.

The First B

Base Clarity — orientation, breath, mind

This is where I apply my master's degree in transpersonal psychology, along with my studies in general semantics, women's spirituality, and creative innovation.

Go deeper: the exploration portal

The foundation of this offering is a donation-based interactive exploration portal where I support people to discover what it means to orient themselves by wellness. I then help people to experience their unique texture of wellness from within through guided visualization. And I leave them with a meditation technique (based in my foundations in transcendental meditation and women's spirituality) supporting them to easefully maintain, nurture, and amplify this internally based felt sense of wellness.

Go deeper: one on one sessions

The progression of Base Clarity from the completion of this portal is a one on one session where we work together through dynamic reciprocity to amplify this sense of wellness inside of one's life with ease — within one's own mind, personal spaces, and relationships. This can be as practical as it is intellectual, based on the need of the one I am working with.

From getting direct access to one's basic needs to filtering the words, thoughts, and stories in one's mind — all with the intention to make one's particular felt sense of wellness as easeful as possible. This is based in my bachelor's studies in sustainability and my life studies homesteading, visiting eco-villages and intentional communities, applying systems towards my lifelong w.i.s.dom efforts. This process can be very simple and can last a lifetime, but every process that brings alignment is a major step forward in this journey of base clarity.

The Second B

Bridge Competence — boundaries, branding, & broadcast

This is where I apply my bachelor's degree in media and communications and my life studies of sovereignty tools and systems.

To bridge competently is to begin to bring your base clarity into the public world as a self-referencing and internally directing being, something akin to growing up into yourself.

Go deeper: the child growing into an adult

A child has to keep their door unlocked because they are still being raised into adulthood — parents need to come into the room, clean up the space, and make sure what they have going on is safe and appropriate. But to be an adult is to manage one's own affairs: to have safes and locked doors protecting the resources (and possibly the family) one stewards and ways of sustaining the lifestyle that maintains one's wellness.

The point: it's healthy to have boundaries that establish us as adults. Yet in society today, almost everything most of us do online — and even offline on our personal devices — is like an open room. The government and the people who own and build the technology can have full access to everything we have and create digitally.

Go deeper: the adult preparing to sustain their life on multiple levels

We are taught that we must make money in order to survive. We are taught that we need to get a job to make that money. However, so few of us are taught that not only do we have the responsibility to make money, we also have the responsibility to maintain our own wellness, to maintain the quality of life that we want to live.

The point: We have to find a way that we can do both: make money and maintain the quality of wellness that we can access now and amplify in the future.

Go deeper: the adult offering what they have prepared w.i.s.ely

So many of us who know that we have something that we want to offer to the world go to the convenient route when we go to offer it. We get websites based in tools that we don't control. We have all of our promotions through social media platforms that we can easily be censored on or kicked off of. And ultimately, the way that we present our own work isn't in our control.

The point: We don't have to do everything by ourselves, but we can at least partner with those who honor the intention we hold to nurture our wellness, honor our interconnection, and maintain our sovereignty as much as possible and support them to continue to grow their tools to be closer and closer to the convenience of the bigger companies while at the same time not having to lose all of our autonomy in the process.

Bridge Competence is a one-on-one collaboration with three offerings:

Boundaries

Establish healthy boundaries. What is yours and what organizes your life — your files, email, calendar, tasks — is privately stewarded by you, accessed only by the organizations you give permission to, permission you can take away at any time, fully.

Branding

Your branding is how you present yourself to the world, in alignment with the way you want to show up — supporting the wellness you may have already so deeply cultivated. Yes, it's your logo, subline, color palette, fonts — and more than anything, it's the clarity of what you're presenting holistically, calling in more of what you are wanting in your life, consciously.

Broadcast

This is where you present to the world what you have clarified as your work, separated from what you keep private. Your website, your offerings, your social presence — built to maintain the sovereignty you've been nurturing.

Go deeper: the website we could create together

The website we could create together would be fully coded from scratch — with only what you need, beautiful and simple, with a functional interface for you to add to it without needing to go into the coding or worry about messing up the design of the site.

And this website can be maintained for very little to no cost. Where most websites can be hundreds a year, this one can be free, or maybe $10 a year if you want a personal domain.

This is also your social media presence: establishing easy rhythms that help grow your presence and anchor it back into your website and email list, so you maintain autonomy with the people you connect with. And connecting you to alternative internet spaces like Qortal and privacy-oriented communication tools like Signal — so that with every step into the world, you are fortifying your foundations and maintaining your base clarity.

The Third B

Bloom Courageously — status, equity, stewardship

This is where I apply my ongoing studies in faith-based living and private law.

To bloom courageously is to step fully into the public world — clear about the ways those spaces offer to honor your sovereignty, and being sure not to agree to anything other than that. You don't have to live a double life where you're w.i.s.e at home and then submit in public to rules that go against your wellness. You can stand fully inside these environments, knowing many of them exist to honor those courageous enough to stand as people who recognize that they are co-creators of their lives and internally directed towards their own w.i.s.dom.

Go deeper: meta-law and stewardship

My mentors approach this work from many angles, ultimately with the same intentions: to be practical in the application, deep and consistent in the fundamental principles, and effective in the results.

Their work recognizes that the law is meta-law — meaning it is created (meta: to transform, create) by the contracts we sign up for. And often within many of these contracts there are more questions than answers. Most of us just sign them presuming the answers are already there — not realizing that we can put our answers in. We don't have to accept theirs.

In the American context specifically, their work presents the option to stand as someone based in their relationship to Source rather than subservience to a government — and, from there, to learn to access and steward the abundance granted by Source, place resources in forms that honor that stewardship, and stand in that status in all spaces over time.

I am only in the beginning of this journey myself. I have had mentors who took me through processes I later realized weren't the right steps to take, sequentially or at all — so I am taking a few backward steps and showing up in this way as a baby right now. I wouldn't try to walk you through this myself, but I can entrust you to my mentors.
